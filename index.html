How did we get here?

Explore a first prototype of an interactive history of reality — from the beginning of the universe toward Earth, life, humanity, and civilization.

~13.8 billion years ago

The Big Bang

The observable universe begins its expansion from an extremely hot, dense early state.

Hundreds of millions of years later

First Stars

Gravity gathers matter into the first stars, creating the environments where heavier elements eventually form.

Early cosmic history

Galaxies

Large structures of stars, gas, dust and dark matter develop throughout the expanding universe.

~4.6 billion years ago

Solar System

Our Sun and planetary system form from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust.

~4.54 billion years ago

Earth

Earth forms and undergoes a violent early history of impacts, melting, differentiation and planetary change.

More than 3 billion years ago

Life

Evidence indicates that life existed on Earth billions of years ago. Its exact origin remains an area of scientific study.

Deep human history

Humans

Hominin evolution eventually produces Homo sapiens and a species capable of spreading across much of the planet.

Holocene

Agriculture

Humans independently domesticate plants and animals in multiple regions, transforming relationships between people and landscapes.

Early civilizations

Cities & Civilization

Permanent settlements, cities, institutions and increasingly complex societies emerge in different regions.
